About

AI Chess is a fully offline Progressive Web App — no servers, no tracking, just chess.

How to play

  • Tap or click a piece to see its legal moves.
  • Drag a piece, or tap a highlighted square, to move.
  • Use Undo / Redo to step through the game.
  • Flip the board to view from the other side.
  • Open Settings to change theme, sound, difficulty and mode.
